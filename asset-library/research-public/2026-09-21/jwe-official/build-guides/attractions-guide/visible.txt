## Creating Appeal
Guests might come to your park to see the dinosaurs, but to be a commercial success and create Appeal, you will need to build Attractions that keep them happy as well. The total Appeal of a park is calculated from the Visible Dinosaurs and Entertainment Attractions combined, but can be reduced by a lack of Species Variety.
### Appeal
Appeal draws Guests to the park and mainly comes from the visible dinosaurs within a park. The higher the Appeal, the more Guests will want to come and visit.
Each species of dinosaur has a unique base Appeal value that is further boosted when they’ve been naturally bred, or if they’re a Juvenile. Well-cared-for Juveniles will also grow into adulthood with an additional boost to their Appeal.
Some Entertainment Attractions provide a static amount of Appeal, but most derive their Appeal from the dinosaurs they can see.
The higher the Appeal of dinosaurs within view of Attractions the higher this will raise the overall guest count in that area. Depending on the Attraction used, different Guest demographics will dominate that local area respectively.
A high total appeal will attract more guests to your park, directly increasing your revenue.
- Ensure your dinosaurs are visible to guests. Build Viewing Galleries and Platforms to increase the number of dinosaurs visible to your guests.
- Synthesise more dinosaurs. Your park will have a minimum Species Target so the wider the variety of dinosaurs in your park, the higher your Appeal bonus. If you don’t reach the Species Target, this negatively affects your Appeal score.
- Breed and care for juvenile dinosaurs. Well cared for juvenile dinosaurs will add to your park’s appeal score both as juveniles and when they become adults.
- Build Entertainment Attractions. Attractions such as the Innovation Centre will have a base Appeal score. Whereas attractions such as the Cretaceous Cruise, Balloon Tour and Dinosaur Encounter derive their Appeal score from the Local Dinosaurs in the area.
### Visibility
Your Park’s Appeal will only be affected by the dinosaurs in view of guests so it’s important to think about visibility when planning the layout of your attractions.
Dinosaur Appeal can only be used to draw guests to the site if they can be seen from a Viewing Attraction. Use the Dinosaur Visibility View Mode to identify any gaps in the coverage of Viewing Attractions.
Keep your dinosaurs’ Comfort in mind as well when thinking about visibility – for example some species will only be comfortable when their nests are out of view.
Viewing Attractions with high Appeal will likely become congested with guests. Plan the site around this with wider Paths and alternative Viewing Attractions to disperse the crowds. Take advantage with Amenities in the same areas to increase site income.
### Did You Know?
Improve the visibility of your dinosaurs by using well-placed Viewing Galleries, Viewing Platforms, Monorails, Hotels, and Tour Rides.
## Building Attractions
Attractions in Jurassic World Evolution 3 will appeal to different guest demographics so, depending on your park, you may favour some more than others.
Learn more about Guest demographics in our Guests Guide .
## Types of Attractions
Coming in different shapes and sizes, Attractions provide appeal for guests by allowing them to see and learn about the dinosaurs in your park.
#### Viewing Platforms & Galleries
Viewing Platforms and Galleries provide different ways for guests to view dinosaurs deep within their enclosures. Different Viewing Platforms and Galleries will provide different sight lines and visibility, so try to build them where they’ll give the best Visibility for your guests. Viewing Platforms and Galleries are most suitable for Nature guests and are also available for Lagoons and Aviaries.
There are many ways for your guests to view dinosaurs in your parks, whether they’re remotely located or hidden from view in enclosures.

#### Dinosaur Encounter
A Viewing Attraction that allows park guests to interact with and feed small to medium-sized herbivores. The building requires its Food supply to be restocked periodically by the Maintenance Team . Not all dinosaurs are compatible with this Attraction, so make sure to double check the Dinosaur Encounter will be suitable before building.
Guests get the chance to interact with some of your park’s friendlier dinosaurs.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Dinosaur Encounter attraction.
- Attach the Dinosaur Encounter building to an existing fenced enclosure or create a new Enclosure starting from the Dinosaur Encounter.
- Populate the Enclosure with suitable dinosaurs, such as juveniles and small herbivores.
- Connect the Dinosaur Encounter to your path network, provide it with Power, and if necessary, resupply it with food.
### Did You Know?
Paths can become overcrowded. Make sure you spread guests out by offering a wide variety of attractions and different routes to get there.
#### Ziplines
Ziplines are a special Viewing Attraction. They give guests visibility of dinosaurs as they travel along a line suspended between two towers. Zipline height will adjust automatically based on the terrain. Zipline direction can be swapped as desired. Ziplines can also improve Transport Rating between the towers, as guests use them to shorten their journeys on foot.
Ziplines are a fun attraction for guests and provide a great view of your dinosaurs.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Zipline attraction.
- Choose the appearance of the Zipline tower or create a Custom Design.
- Place the entry Zipline tower, then the exit Zipline tower, swapping the height in the info panel.
- Connect both towers to your path network and provide them with Power.
#### Tours
Tours are a type of Viewing Attraction that uses vehicles to gain dinosaur visibility along a tour route. They can also improve the Transport Rating of the site by chaining multiple tours together, station to station.
For some tours, dinosaurs can pose a danger to the guests by attacking tour vehicles. Assign Capture Teams to Ranger Posts to automatically Tranquilise dinosaurs that pose a threat to vehicles within range of that post.
Take guests on the ride of a lifetime through dinosaur enclosures with Tour attractions.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select either the Park Tour or Jurassic Tour attraction.
- Choose the appearance of the Tour Station building or create a Custom Design.
- Plot the route for your tour through your existing enclosures. When plotting the tour through a fence or path it will automatically select a Tour Gate or Vehicle crossing.
- Connect the start and end of the tour to your Tour Station. Connect the Tour Station building to your path network and provide it with Power.
Alternatively, build safer tour routes by making sure there is a fence - or at least a good distance - between the tour vehicles and the dinosaurs. Tracked tour rides provide dinosaur visibility within and around enclosures, making them most suitable for Adventure guests.
Gyrospheres are a dynamic tour experience that enables guests to get up close to your dinosaurs.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Gyrosphere attraction and place the Gyrosphere Station or create a Custom Design.
- Plot the route for your Gyrosphere through your existing enclosures. When plotting through a fence or path it will automatically select an available Gate to keep your enclosures secure.
- Connect the start and end of the track to your Gyrosphere station. Ensure the building is connected to your path network and provide it with Power.
#### Activity Centres
Activity Centres are a type of Entertainment Attraction and provide a set amount of Appeal to the park. Activity Centres can be customised to attract different guest Demographics to the local area. This can help with setting up and maximising profits from Amenities.
Attract guests with particular interests to specific areas of your park using Activity Centres.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Activity Centre building.
- Choose the Appearance of the Activity Centre building or create a Custom Design.
- Select the type of Attraction in the info panel with Edit Attraction. Note which Guest Demographics will be interested in this Activity Centre.
- Connect the Activity Centre to your path network and provide it with Power.
#### Innovation Centres
An educational entertainment experience where technology meets prehistory, Innovation Centres are a type of Entertainment Attraction and provide a set amount of Appeal. Innovation Centres attracts guests with specific preferences and can be customised to suit the interests of your park.
Attract guests with educational entertainment by building an Innovation Centre.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Innovation Centre building.
- Choose an Appearance for the Innovation Centre or create a Custom Design.
- Connect all entrances to your path network and provide it with Power.
### Did You Know?
Concentrate your guest amenities around popular Viewing Galleries, Attractions, Hotels and Monorail Stations to maximise your income.
#### Amphitheatres
A stunning facility that showcases your dinosaurs in a state-of-the-art setting, the Amphitheatre provides dinosaur visibility for park guests and is best suited for large carnivores. As with other viewing attractions, dinosaurs only appeal to guests if they have been recently visible.
Allow guests to marvel at the spectacle of dinosaurs with a grand Amphitheatre.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Amphitheatre building or create a Custom Design.
- Attach security fences at the Amphitheatre opening to connect to enclosures and keep your dinosaurs secure.
- Connect the Amphitheatre to your path network and provide it with Power.
#### Cretaceous Cruise
The Cretaceous Cruise is a special Viewing Attraction and Tour. It is able to send guests onto waterways in kayaks, where they can get close to dinosaurs at the site.
Unlike ground-based Tours, the Cretaceous Cruise routes must be plotted over water, and the Tour building must be placed at least partially in water too.
Allow guests to get up close to your dinosaurs in this water attraction.
- Open the Environment menu and place a body of water using the Shallow Water or Deep Water brush.
- Open the Attractions menu and place the Cretaceous Cruise building, choosing the Appearance or creating a Custom Design. Ensure the dock is in water and the entrance is on land.
- Ensure the dock is in water and the entrance is on land.
- Place the tour route through the water, connecting the start and finish to the dock.
- Connect the Cretaceous Cruise building to your path network and provide it with Power.
#### Balloon Tours
Balloon Tours are a special Viewing Attraction and Tour. They are able to send guests airbourne, where they can view dinosaurs from a completely safe vantage point.
Unlike ground-based Tours, Balloon Tour routes can travel over buildings and obstacles. However, each Balloon Tour can only support a single Balloon, and they are very slow in their movement.
Guests can take to the sky and get a pterosaurs' view of your park on the Balloon Tour.
- Open the Attractions menu.
- Select the Balloon Tour building.
- Choose the Appearance of the Balloon Tour building or create a Custom Design.
- Place the Balloon Tour route, connecting the start and end to the tour building.
- Connect the Balloon Tour building to your path network and provide it with Power.
