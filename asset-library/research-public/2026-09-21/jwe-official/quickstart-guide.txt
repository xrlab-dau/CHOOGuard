Introduction
Get to grips with the basics of Jurassic World Evolution 3
Welcome park manager!
Jurassic World Evolution 3 offers the ultimate dinosaur park management experience, with lots to learn if you want to run your own successful park. But what do you need to know before jumping in?
What is Jurassic World Evolution 3?
Jurassic World Evolution 3 is the next entry in the award-winning series of dinosaur park construction and management games, introducing modular building, dimorphism, natural breeding and juvenile dinosaurs to the series for the very first time.
In Jurassic World Evolution 3 you’ll be building your very own Jurassic World across multiple gameplay modes, constructing and running your park while tackling the challenges associated with bringing prehistoric animals into our modern world.
Getting Started
Whether you’re a veteran park manager or venturing into the world of dinosaur park management for the first time, there are a few options for getting started.
Watch Our Welcome Video
The Welcome video provides a whistle stop tour of the modes, game mechanics and tools you’ll be using in Jurassic World Evolution 3. This is a fantastic starting point and will kit you out with everything you need to know to kickstart your career as a park manager.
Try the Campaign
Jurassic World Evolution 3 features an expansive globetrotting campaign which will see you managing parks and facilities around the world. The campaign has been tailored to gradually introduce players to the various mechanics in Jurassic World Evolution 3 and is perfect for getting to grips and learning to play, before diving into the Challenge or Sandbox modes.
Read Our Guides
To assist both new and returning players, we’ve created loads of helpful guides and how-tos to help keep you right. If you’re ever stuck, try reading the relevant guide or accessing the handy “Help” section in-game.
Did You Know?
You can find Jurassic World Evolution 3’s controls and hotkeys on our website or in-game.
Building a Dinosaur Park
Jurassic World Evolution 3 gives you the freedom to construct your own dinosaur park, making use of a huge range of attractions, blueprints, facilities and modular building options.
Crafting your park can be as simple or as granular as you want, so it’s down to you to make the choice in how you play with the available construction tools.
Of course, your adult and juvenile dinosaurs will be the stars of the show, so you’ll need to make sure you build enclosures that meet their needs and preferences.
Did You Know?
A great park is a profitable park – read the Finances Guide to learn more about how to manage your park finances in Jurassic World Evolution 3.
Running Your Site
In Jurassic World Evolution 3 you have an arsenal of management tools at your disposal. You’ll be responsible for keeping guests happy, while also managing your workforce of scientists and running your parks’ operations.
Knowing the ins and outs of park management is just the beginning though… Chaos is inevitable when running a dinosaur park and you’ll need to know how to react to the calamities, sabotage and dinosaur escapes that may occur.
Did You Know?
As a park manager you have a fleet of vehicles that you can call on to help maintain and run your park.
Help Life Find a Way
Now we’ve equipped you with the tools and resources you need to create your own successful park. Of course, the best way to learn is to play yourself, so head on over and get started!
For more helpful Jurassic World Evolution 3 guides, tips & tricks and other information, browse the website, follow our social channels and subscribe to the newsletter.
