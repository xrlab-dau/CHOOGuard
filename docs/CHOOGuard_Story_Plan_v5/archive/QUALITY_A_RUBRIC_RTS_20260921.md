# A 품질 고정 기준

CS-EXEC.01.01 · A-fixed-1 · 사용자 요청: A가 될 때까지 지속 개선

8영역 모두 근거와 실행 결과로 PASS일 때만 전체 A로 판정한다. 미검증·실패 항목을 평균 점수로 덮지 않는다. 현재 판정은 NOT_A이며 수치 점수를 임의로 부여하지 않는다.

게임 품질(G1–G6), 계산 검증/검수(G7), 실제 현장 보정(G8)을 구분한다. 공개자료의 정확도·수집 시점과 추정 범위를 기록한다. 조건을 낮춰 A를 만들지 않으며 기준 변경은 이유와 이전 기준을 보존한다.

## G1 Playable operational loop

Three distinct 20-30 minute native sessions: ordinary escalation, poor response, competing incidents. Same citizens continue into recovery without instant failure cutoff. Paired choices change coverage, stock, readiness and later decisions; independent review confirms sustained decisions.

현재: PARTIAL: hall evacuation/return; full sessions and recurrent choices absent.

## G2 Benchmark and market application

Source-to-runtime matrix for facility/team/target, capacity, resource competition, recovery and world-context UI. Relevant runtime placement of temporary response facilities/controls with undo and edited-state recovery; copied benchmark mechanics must fit emergency operations. Source counts or private-engine/pixel-parity claims earn no credit.

현재: PARTIAL: 8 commercial/3 OSS comparisons; port runs, management/building/operator-fit missing.

## G3 UI and control usability

Independent input-handler session/frame review at supported resolutions: selection/request/invalid target/cancel/follow/zoom/floors/debrief usable without internal knowledge. Korean UI, disabled reasons, readable world; no public graph/probabilities. Bind claims to tested input surface; no OS/human parity inference.

현재: PARTIAL PASS: rays/callbacks/invalid target/floor context; full-session learnability/readability missing.

## G4 Modeling and animation quality

All station floors/principal base interiors usable. Metre scale for people/vehicles/doors/furniture. Close/mid/far and movement review: coherent materials, recognizable roles, walk/work/wait/return, no blocking clipping/placeholder core sites. Record asset sources and measured/authored dimensions.

현재: PROTOTYPE: three exteriors/simple people; city mostly simple geometry.

## G5 Continuous world and performance

Travel/dispatch across Station-Choryang-Jungang-Nampo-North Port with road/floor connectivity and blocked-route handling. Geodata/control-point uncertainties documented. Declared hardware/resolution, full-session traversal: no crash/stall/unbounded growth; p95 frame time <=33.3 ms.

현재: PARTIAL: metre city; full traversal/performance absent.

## G6 Internal logic and durable state

Accepted outcomes drive transitions; evacuation is not medical recovery. Pause/retry/duplicate/stale responses/reservations preserve invariants. Save/restart/replay restore citizens/incidents/commands/stock/outcomes. Jev defaults/provenance explicit; no late retroactive changes.

현재: PARTIAL PASS: Jev equation/pause/fallback; two teams ready sim443. Replay/resources/medical outcome missing.

## G7 Computational verification and validation

Pinned fire/smoke and crowd/bottleneck/contact-load models pass applicable published verification/validation cases with predeclared source-supported tolerances; show convergence, units/time/coupling and uncertainty. Medical response procedures and any claimed health effects have supporting models/data. No clinical efficacy is required unless claimed or needed by modeled outcomes, and no unsupported clinical/physical result may be reported as proven.

현재: NOT_VERIFIED: solvers/reference hall do not prove coupled predictive accuracy.

## G8 Real-site calibration

Public dated sources constrain station/base geometry, exits, materials, ventilation and occupancy with uncertainty. Independent observed site data validate claimed site outcomes against predeclared tolerances. Plans are not assumed as-built; unsupported interior/clinical inputs cannot pass.

현재: NOT_VERIFIED: no supplied as-built or sufficient site calibration; remains a gap even if other tracks pass.
