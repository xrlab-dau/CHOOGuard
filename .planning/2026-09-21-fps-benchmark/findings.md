# Findings

Research findings will be recorded here after each source pass. Treat source claims as bounded by the cited page, manual, or footage locator.

## Source pass 1

- PUBG official support lists the explicit movement/action vocabulary: WASD, crouch, prone, jump/vault, sprint, walk, lean, view/aim controls (`https://support.pubg.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002074913-What-are-the-basic-game-commands`, Movement/View tables). Official Update 15.2 defines crosshair-to-world/map ping and says the intent is faster, clearer teammate communication (`https://pubg.com/en-ca/news/1725`, QoL Improvements > Screen Ping Marker). Official game overview defines the shrinking Blue Zone as a health-decreasing pressure that forces movement (`https://pubg.com/en/game-info/overview`, Blue Zone). Transfer: embodied stance/pace and diegetic world-pointing; replace combat zone damage with blocked/unsafe-route urgency and visible passenger state. Do not transfer looting, weapons, or last-survivor incentives.
- Sudden Attack official guide provides unusually exact attention/UI evidence: radio message hotkey; radio-aligned character motion; configurable footstep focus; grenade warning; hit marker; minimap feedback when own footsteps reveal the player (`https://guide.sa.nexon.com/guide/48`, table lines 62-92 as retrieved). Transfer only the pattern of redundant audio + small spatial UI + visible acknowledgement. Combat hit/HP/enemy-reveal systems are forbidden analogues for passengers.
- Ready or Not developer briefing Vol.82 describes a research/blockout/alpha workflow, then door, cover, and interactable passes plus sightline triangles and active-situation route choice (`https://voidinteractive.net/vol-82-ready-or-not-development-briefing/`, Stage 3 and pass bullets). Vol.90 says civilian behavior/compliance can be tuned, and describes how open vs locked doors alter sightlines and forced entry burden (`https://voidinteractive.net/vol-90-ready-or-not-development-briefing/`, Civilian AI Modifiers and Miscellaneous Modifiers). Transfer: uncertain NPC response, doorway/sightline legibility, alternate routes, and authored recovery. Police compliance, weapons, breaching and suspect AI are combat-role-only and must not migrate.
- Alien: Isolation GDC Vault's official session overview establishes the design target: one meaningful encounter with an underpowered/unprepared player; the creature uses senses; visual and aural immersion are critical (`https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1021852/Building-Fear-in-Aliens`, overview lines 32-43). The overview alone does not prove detailed AI mechanics or inputs, so claims stay at the pressure/information-asymmetry level unless footage/slides provide exact locators.
- Half-Life: Alyx official page states direct embodied actions (rummage shelves, manipulate tools, pull/throw objects) and supports varied play environments, locomotion methods and input devices (`https://www.half-life.com/en/alyx`, Full-length game section). Valve's official commentary update describes an object-shaped instruction: grab a headset, put it on, off-hand panel shows progress/speaker, remove to stop, drop resets; subtitles include Korean (`https://steamcommunity.com/games/546560/announcements/detail/2957137317359486740`, Commentary Update). Transfer: world-object affordance, hand-near-object feedback, reversible action and multimodal text. Do not transfer VR-only hand precision as a mouse/keyboard usability fact.
- Firefighting Simulator - The Squad official astragon page states AI/human cooperation, varied fires, dynamic spread, coordination, caution, and rescue; PlayStation official page says single-player player leads an AI team and can assign tasks (`https://www.astragon.com/news/detail/firefighting-simulator-the-squad`; `https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/firefighting-simulator-the-squad/`). Transfer: bounded task assignment with visible teammate execution and resource/tool dependencies. It models US firefighters, so suppression, entry, apparatus and command authority are not station-staff procedure evidence.

## Evidence limits

- None of the six games was executed locally. Evidence is official documentation, official developer communication, and bounded official footage/page descriptions.
- A page title or trailer description is not enough for a fine-grained input-to-feedback causal claim. Exact mechanics use table/section locators; timestamp claims will be included only when footage itself is inspected.
- These references reveal abstraction patterns, not KORAIL procedural authority, human-factors validation, or CHOOGuard product acceptance.

## Synthesis

- The six sources converge on a repeatable abstraction chain: situated verb -> explicit constraint -> immediate world feedback -> visible downstream actor/state -> recoverable correction.
- Combat framing is unnecessary to retain the useful mechanics. The safe transfer set is point-and-acknowledge reporting, source-authorized operational windows, compact attention UI, sightline-aware passenger guidance, observable response states, attributed partial information, forgiving world-object interaction, visible delegation and explicit resource dependencies.
- The major risks are weapon/tool reskins, omniscient radar, fake smoke/health physics, police/firefighter authority transfer and invisible mission-flag completion.
- Root/Jev should reject any mission proposal that lacks KORAIL authority, exposes hidden ground truth, uses unexplained random failure, or makes station staff suppress/clear/fight.
