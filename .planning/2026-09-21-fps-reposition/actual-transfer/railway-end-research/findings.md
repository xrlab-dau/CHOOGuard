# Findings

## Known primary constraints accepted as navigation
- 2019 Busan City material: the passage enters the railway station at first-floor/plaza level; lateral escalator circulation continues toward the second-floor railway concourse.
- 2021 BISCO operator CAD: two central 1200-type escalators plus flanking stairs at the KTX end; the other escalator pair belongs to the bus-center spur.
- 2018-405: 4.37 m and 5 m are clear heights, not depth or escalator rise.

# Interim findings

- BISCO's 2023 work-plan PDF identifies `부산역지하연결통로 에스컬레이터 3,4호기`, says they were installed in 2015, and scopes replacement of four handrails. This distinguishes the early pair from the later railway-end equipment, but it does not state travel height or landing elevation.
- BISCO's live contract database returns passage-specific maintenance contracts (including 2024 drive-wheel and step-roller work and a 2025 step-chain replacement). The public contract-detail pages expose commercial metadata but no design attachment or travel-height field.
- No public KORAIL/KRIC Busan Station floor-plan result found so far. KRIC exposes station basics only.
- A contemporaneous NewsBusan report explicitly separates the underground-passage arrival at railway-station 1F from the lateral station escalators that continue to the 2F waiting hall. This is useful topology, but it is secondary evidence and supplies no metric rise.
- Public video metadata names the passage elevator model/manufacturer, but it is non-primary and is being used only as a registry-search lead.

## Guardrails

- Do not interpret the published 4.37 m or 5 m values as depth or escalator rise; they are clear heights.
- Do not infer travel height from the `1200형` escalator designation.
- Keep the passage-end escalators distinct from the separate lateral 1F-to-2F station escalators described in the 2019 report.
