# KTX railway-end landing research

## Goal
Find primary, source-supported constraints for the KTX Busan Station end of the 2019 underground passage: first-floor connection mouth/landing, relationship to the second-floor concourse, and escalator travel height or landing elevation. If no primary source publishes an exact rise, document a bounded negative after covering the relevant official resource classes.

## Scope
- New gap only; do not redo Metro/mall inventory research.
- Read-only research. Write only in this directory.
- No Unity, Assets, scenes, code, configuration, or Editor interaction.
- Do not derive rise from escalator width/type or passage clear height.

## Evidence classes
- `directly_supported`: exact primary plan, record, caption, registry, or tender supports the claim.
- `contradicts_premise`: primary evidence rules out a stated/model assumption.
- `near_match_only`: official but does not resolve the exact rise/anchor.
- `insufficient`: relevant primary-source classes were covered without the missing dimension.
