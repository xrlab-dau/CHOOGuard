## First seam-source pass
- The existing official-metro graph classifies the exact HUMETRO→BISCO seam as insufficient. It has no shared control point, labelled wall opening, or fare-gate coordinate; its BISCO arrow must remain non-directional.
- A current HUMETRO page about connection-corridor installation describes the approval/design process and says the principle is connection to an existing entrance. Its facility table reports zero `지하상가` cases on Line 1. That does not disprove the older Busan Station mall/passage topology, but it prevents treating this generic current program table as proof of a HUMETRO-managed mall seam.
- Current BISCO facility inventory records Busan Station underground mall at Dong-gu Jungang-daero underground 200, with 82 shops, and the separate 99.6 m connection passage. It still does not map an exact interface.

## Stronger topology and current-use evidence
- A 2015 International Newspaper report says a partial Busan Station connection segment opened from the bus stop by escalator directly to Metro, while BISCO managed the 1994 Busan Station underground mall. The report explicitly describes the project as connecting railway Busan Station and Metro Busan Station underground. It provides historic functional continuity but its pre-completion dimensions/facility counts cannot replace the 2019 as-built release.
- A first-person Feat.coco post published 2025-12-13 describes the BISCO-operated Busan Station underground-mall Open Business Center as located in an underground mall `붙어있는` to Metro Busan Station. This is later current-use adjacency evidence, not yet an exact boundary/landmark because the post's images are not inspected.
