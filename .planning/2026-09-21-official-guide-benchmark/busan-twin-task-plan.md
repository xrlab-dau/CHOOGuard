# Busan digital-twin public source discovery

- Scope: public, read-only evidence for Busan railway station and the Busan Station–Nampo–North Port corridor.
- Existing baseline: OSM footprints/rail/roads/coastline and Mapzen elevation are already implemented; do not rediscover them.
- Deliverable: `busan-twin-source-graph.json`, at most eight high-value candidates, exact access/rights/status, one usable next acquisition or explicit unavailability, and a Blender/Unity reconstruction recipe.
- Evidence standard: official primary sources first; source-creator pages for shared models; distinguish visible imagery, downloadable geometry, API availability, licensing, and assumptions.
- Boundaries: no computer use, account creation, keys, auth bypass, installs, large downloads, or claims that photographs provide measured dimensions.

## Work lanes

1. Inspect local spatial evidence and the current Blender/Unity pipeline.
2. Verify official 3D/geospatial data services and licensing.
3. Verify railway-station topology and authoritative public imagery.
4. Check source-creator shared models and overlapping public imagery.
5. Synthesize a bounded source graph and validate JSON.
